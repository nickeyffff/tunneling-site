Strata Tuning

G1 / G2 / G4 Cutter Ring Materials

Three steel grades — strong wear resistance (G1), conventional (G2), high toughness (G4) — matched to full-face hard rock, all strata and mixed ground.

Cutter rings use custom "Great Wall Special Steel": electric-furnace melting, ladle refining, vacuum degassing and electroslag remelting (ESR) (P < 0.015%, S < 0.005%) — far below the national high-quality mold-steel requirement of 0.03%.
